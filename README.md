## Packages & Add-ons
- Weekly regular slot (same time each week): 10% discount on listed hourly rates.  
- Block booking (10+ hours prepaid): 12% discount.  
- First-time band special: 15% off your first 2-hour booking.  
- Multitrack rehearsal recording (capture your session): $45/hr (includes basic stereo mixdown).  
- On-call engineer / tech: $40/hr.  
- Extra guitar / amp hire or upgraded kit on request — prices vary.  
- Drum maintenance / excessive setup fee: $30 (if requested or heavy use).

**Important:** **The 20% block/half-day/full-day discount does NOT apply to recording fees, multitrack recording, or engineer/tech fees — those services are charged at their listed rates and are excluded from the 20% block discount.**

## Booking terms (short)
- Minimum booking: 1 hour (2 hours recommended for new bands).  
- Cancellation: Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before start; less than 48 hours — 50% refund.  
- Security: First-time bookings require a valid card hold or bond.  
- Insurance: Bands are responsible for their gear; public liability coverage recommended.
