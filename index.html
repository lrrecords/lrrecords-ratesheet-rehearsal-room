Booking terms (short)

  • Minimum booking: 1 hour (2 hours recommended for new bands).
  • Cancellation: Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before start; less than 48 hours — 50% refund.
  • Security: First-time bookings require a valid card hold or bond.
  • Insurance: Bands are responsible for their gear; public liability coverage recommended.

Additional fees (explicit)

  • Multitrack rehearsal recording (includes basic stereo mixdown): $45/hr (no 20% block discount).
  • On-call engineer / tech: $40/hr (no 20% block discount).
  • Extra guitar / amp hire or upgraded kit: prices vary — request quote.
  • Drum maintenance / excessive setup fee: $30 (flat if requested or heavy use).
